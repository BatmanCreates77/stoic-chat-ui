      What this can and cannot measure
      
    
        
  • Gamma, grey tracking, contrast ratio, uniformity, flicker: reliable in relative terms when the phone supports RAW and manual exposure.
    • 
        
  • White point and gamut: uses the camera's own colour matrix. Expect a few hundred kelvin of uncertainty and treat gamut coverage as indicative.
    • 
        
  • Absolute brightness in nits: not possible without a reference. Enter a known white luminance in the report to scale the numbers.
    • 
        
  • Deep black: the phone's noise floor is far above a colorimeter's, so OLED contrast is reported as a lower bound.
